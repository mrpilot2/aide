    Notifications

    The application uses notifications to tell you about events, such as a task that has finished or a problem that needs your attention. The Notifications page of the Settings dialog controls how notifications are shown.

    Open the page

    1. Choose the Settings action. It is usually in the File menu.
    2. In the Settings dialog, select Appearance & Behavior and then Notifications.

    Balloons and the notification log

    A notification can appear as a balloon, a small popup in a corner of the screen:

    • A Balloon closes by itself after a short time. A progress bar shows the remaining time, and it pauses while the mouse pointer is over the balloon.
    • A Sticky balloon stays open until you close it.

    Every notification is also recorded in the notification log, whether or not it appeared as a balloon. If the application shows the notification log, you can read missed notifications there.

    Do Not Disturb

    Select Do Not Disturb to stop all balloons. Notifications are still recorded in the notification log. Clear the check box to show balloons again.

    Balloon placement

    Balloon placement selects the corner of the screen where balloons appear: Bottom right, Bottom left, Top right or Top left. When several balloons are open, they stack from that corner towards the middle of the screen.

    Notification groups

    Each notification belongs to a group, for example all notifications of one feature. The table lists every group of the application:

    • Group is the name of the group.
    • Popup type selects how the group's notifications appear: None (only in the notification log), Balloon or Sticky balloon.
    • Log shows that the group's notifications are recorded in the notification log.

    To find a group quickly, type its name into the search field at the top of the Settings dialog. Matching groups are highlighted.

    Apply your changes

    Changes take effect when you click Apply or OK. Cancel closes the dialog and discards changes that you have not applied. While the page has unapplied changes, a Reset link restores the values that are currently in effect.

    New settings apply to notifications that arrive afterwards. Notifications that were already shown keep their original appearance.
